About MSAS

Analytics belongs to everyone.

A student-led community at the University of Michigan where sports become a practical way to learn statistics, data science, and analytical thinking.

Who we are

Built for curious students.

We are a student-led organization at the University of Michigan dedicated to helping students develop skills in statistics, data science, and analytical thinking through sports.

We believe anyone can learn analytics, which is why we welcome students of all experience levels. Our members gain hands-on experience working with real sports data through workshops and tutorials in Python and R.

We collaborate with the School of Information and the Sport Management Program in the School of Kinesiology to connect members with valuable resources and opportunities both on and off campus.

Each year, we host the Michigan Sports Analytics Symposium, bringing together students, faculty, and industry professionals who are passionate about sports analytics.
